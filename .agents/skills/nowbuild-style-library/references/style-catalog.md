# Style catalog

NowBuild ships twenty initial visual systems. Brand-referenced entries translate broadly documented design principles; they are not official templates or replicas.

| ID | Direction | Core rules | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| apple-glass | Precision Glass | layered translucent material, disciplined spacing, large quiet type, restrained motion | premium AI, consumer productivity |
| notion-editorial | Document Warmth | warm paper, compact utility navigation, content-first blocks, calm typography | knowledge, collaboration, writing |
| stripe-gradient | Gradient Commerce | technical grid, vivid spectral accent, strong conversion hierarchy | fintech, API, payments |
| linear-dark | Focused Dark | cool near-black, hairline borders, dense keyboard-first UI, subtle glow | developer and productivity tools |
| vercel-mono | Monochrome Launch | black/white, mono details, hard grid, sharp proof and deploy states | developer platforms |
| rams-functional | Less but Better | strict functional hierarchy, one accent, precise 8px rhythm | B2B tools and operations |
| swiss-grid | International Grid | asymmetric columns, bold type scale, rules and color blocks | agencies and data products |
| japanese-ma | Quiet Ma | intentional negative space, warm neutrals, hairlines, slow fades | wellness and premium services |
| scandinavian | Human Utility | warm white, friendly type, soft functional cards, gentle depth | team and consumer SaaS |
| neo-brutalist | Raw Signal | exposed grid, thick borders, hard shadows, direct copy | creator and youth products |
| art-deco | Geometric Prestige | symmetry, architectural frames, deep colors and metallic accent | luxury and events |
| neo-memphis | Playful Motion | broken grid, bold flat colors, expressive shapes and tactile motion | education and creative tools |
| creator-collage | Creator Collage | layered work, handwritten annotations, tactile paper composition | creators and artists |
| portfolio-editorial | Portfolio Issue | magazine spread, dramatic serif, caption-led case studies | designers and architects |
| cinematic-photo | Cinematic Portrait | full-bleed imagery, title-sequence type, minimal chrome | photographers and directors |
| consultant-proof | Proof-led Expert | outcomes before biography, clear offer, credible case evidence | consultants and coaches |
| developer-terminal | Terminal Identity | CLI narrative, shipping log, code-first proof | developers and indie hackers |
| writer-journal | Writer Journal | long-form rhythm, issue archive, quiet editorial voice | writers and journalists |
| academic-folio | Research Folio | citation hierarchy, searchable index, dense clarity | academics and researchers |
| canva-anti-design | Bold Personal Poster | oversized type, clashing color, irregular poster composition | fashion and Gen-Z personal brands |

Source inspiration: Apple Human Interface Guidelines; Notion content styling guidance; Canva portfolio/template guidance; open-source one-page portfolio, Astrofolio, al-folio and modern developer portfolio patterns; Dieter Rams, Swiss/International, Japanese minimalism, Scandinavian, Brutalist, Art Deco, Neo-Memphis and editorial traditions. Product names are used only to help users recognize a design direction.
